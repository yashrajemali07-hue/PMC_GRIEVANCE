Environmental Awareness

Knowledge is the first step towards a sustainable future. Read, learn, and act.

♻️ Waste Segregation

🟢
Wet / Organic Waste
  • Kitchen food scraps and vegetable peels
  • Leftover cooked food and fruit waste
  • Tea/coffee grounds, eggshells
  • Garden trimmings and fallen leaves
Dispose in Green Bin 🟢 — Composted to produce manure
🔵
Dry / Recyclable Waste
  • Paper, newspapers, cardboard
  • Plastic bottles, bags, containers
  • Glass bottles and jars
  • Metal cans, tins, foils
Dispose in Blue Bin 🔵 — Sent to recycling facilities
🔴
Hazardous Waste
  • Batteries and electronic items
  • Medicines and medical waste
  • Chemicals, paints, solvents
  • Used syringes and sharp objects
Dispose in Red Bin 🔴 — Handled by special disposal units
💡 Quick Tips for Waste Management
🛍️
Use cloth bags

Carry reusable bags while shopping to reduce single-use plastic waste.

🏠
Home composting

Set up a simple compost bin at home to turn kitchen waste into garden manure.

📱
E-waste drives

Participate in local e-waste collection drives for old phones and electronics.

🚯
Never litter

Use public dustbins and encourage others to keep public spaces clean.

🌬️ Pollution Reduction

🌫️
Air Pollution
  • Use public transport or carpool
  • Switch to CNG/electric vehicles
  • Avoid burning waste or leaves
  • Plant trees — they filter air naturally
  • Use fans before switching on AC
🏭
Water Pollution
  • Never dump chemicals into drains
  • Use eco-friendly cleaning products
  • Report sewage leaks through PMC Smart Environmental Cell
  • Avoid using pesticides near water bodies
  • Participate in river/lake clean-up drives
🌿
Soil Pollution
  • Avoid using plastic in gardens
  • Use organic compost over chemical fertilisers
  • Report illegal dumping through PMC Smart Environmental Cell
  • Support zero-waste packaging initiatives
  • Grow a small kitchen garden at home
