Waste Segregation at Source

Pune Municipal Corporation mandates 100% segregated waste handover to Swach collectors.

🟢 Wet Waste (Green Bin)

Kitchen food waste, vegetable peels, fruit rinds, tea leaves, garden leaves and leftover food.

🔵 Dry Waste (Blue Bin)

Cardboard, newspapers, plastic wrappers, milk pouches, glass bottles, metal tins and clean packaging.

🔴 Domestic Hazardous & E-Waste

Expired medicines, discarded batteries, tube lights, sanitary waste, electronics and chemical containers.
